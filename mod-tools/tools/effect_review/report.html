How to review

Use Effect outcomes to inspect a shared rule across all linked records. In Conversions, choose Good, Bad, or Partial, then open a record. The detail shows the resulting behavior, adaptations, dropped parts, and original data.

Set your review to Accepted or Needs changes, add notes, then save. Notes are stored with this conversion run. Export a copy whenever you want. Accepting an entry records your review; it does not create or install abilities. You can review groups and give overall approval in conversation; you do not need to mark every source record individually.

What happens next

After your review, the accepted rules and corrections can produce the new unit ability list. This report contains draft mechanics with source identities and dependencies. Destination IDs, commands, animation integration, and gameplay verification belong to that later step.
